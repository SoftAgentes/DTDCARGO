    入仓注意事项 · Reglas de ingreso a bodega

    1. 1. 每个箱子写客户唛头；缺唛头会延误出口。 Mark every carton with the client's Shipping Mark. Without it, the export is delayed.
    2. 2. 发货前填写本单，随货发送，或在你的 DTD 链接上传。 Fill in this form before shipping and send it with the cargo, or upload it on your DTD link.
    3. 3. 货物如果是牌子货，请如实写上品牌名，装柜时我们会特殊处理；如果不是牌子货，请写"无"。如有品牌隐瞒不报，一旦海关查验，后果由供方承担。 If the goods are branded, declare the real brand name (we handle it specially when loading); if not, write 无 (none). Hiding a brand = the supplier bears the consequences of any customs inspection.
    4. 4. 请供货方自行安排卸货及承担卸货费用。如需我司仓库安排工人卸货，则会按照货物体积重量收取相应的卸货费用，实报实销。卸货如需叉车，叉车由义乌港统一收费，一车50元，费用由供应商支付。 The supplier arranges and pays for unloading. If our warehouse provides workers, the unloading fee is charged by volume/weight (actual cost). If a forklift is needed, Yiwu Port charges a flat ¥50 per truck, paid by the supplier.
    5. 5. 违禁品及需许可货物（如电池需 MSDS + CCC），无相应许可证不予收货。 Restricted goods will not be received without their permits (e.g. batteries: MSDS + CCC).
    6. 6. 委内瑞拉联系人（如有疑问）： Venezuelan contact (questions): 微信 DTDLogistic
